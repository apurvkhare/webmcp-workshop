# Slides

The deck is in Google Slides: [Hands-on WebMCP · GDG Cloud Bengaluru DevFest](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Nbw0a8c896K7tpBYQ7PWC8P93FL8LWfXEEbCeamD1Kg/edit). It is private until its owner shares it.

`slides.md` in this folder is the outline the deck was made from, and the source of its speaker notes. Change the deck in Google Slides, and keep this outline in step when the content changes.

- **26 slides.** The outline's 18, plus "About me", the agenda and a picture of where the tools run. Each hands-on section (2 to 6) is two slides in the deck: a divider, then "what you'll fill in".
- **Speaker notes** are on every slide, taken from `slides.md`. The added slides have short notes of their own.
- **Clock times** come from `AGENDA.md`. Change the agenda first, then the outline, then the deck.
- **The look** is the "Editorial Ink" style of the MCP Apps workshop deck: ink background, clay accent, Newsreader headings, Inter for text, Fira Code for code.
- **Pictures:** three screenshots of the finished shop (the drawer's tool list, Agent view, the `place_order` dialog). The dialog shows the next day's delivery slot, so its date is the day after the capture.
- **API details** on the slides were checked in Chrome 157. Check Chrome, or `shop/src/webmcp.d.ts`, before changing one.
- **No numbers.** Chrome has published no comparison, so no slide has step counts, token counts or timings.
